## B. Supplement: concrete mathematical construction

**Gabriel Peyre, Optimal Transport for Dummies / Beginners**, 2017, 29-page Keynote-produced PDF. Mathematical teaching, not a thesis defense or empirical paper report. Academic design is not synonymous with Beamer.

- Repository: https://github.com/gpeyre/2017-ot-beginners
- PDF: https://github.com/gpeyre/2017-ot-beginners/blob/master/slides/slides.pdf
- Inspected pages: 4, 6, 9, 11, 14.

Pages 4 and 6 show one assignment through geography, a bipartite graph and a cost matrix. Red/blue consistently identify the two indexed sets. Selected edges and matrix entries explain the objective. Page 9 visualizes factorial counting through decreasing choices. Page 11 holds composition stable while comparing assignment costs. Page 14 introduces a concrete one-dimensional case before sorting.

Transfer correspondence across representations, consistent symbol coloring, concrete-before-abstract exposition, and stable comparisons. Do not copy p.14's density, edge-to-edge type, saturated palette or map assets. A beginner lecture does not supply the research argument of a seminar.
