PaperStage is a portable skill consumed by the user's existing AI assistant. It has no separate model router, hosted service, local model or browser application. Its emphasis is source-led research narration, institution-aware design choices, honest review gates and small local deterministic helpers. These distinctions are product choices, not evidence of superiority over the above systems.
